My tooling is still shifting almost every week. This round:

Said goodbye to Claude Code via their subscription — still think Claude Code works best with their own models. Fable is really good; Opus 5 leaves me cold.

Codex is the "try, bail, try again" app for me. Never quite landed, and honestly that's fine.

Main stack today is Herdr + Grok Build CLI. SuperGrok Heavy is $300 and that's a hard price to swallow, but the terminal quality is first-class — no question. Herdr is the one worth flagging: it feels like a tmux replacement built for the age of persistent state across machines, and you can actually control the agent itself, not just the terminal. The worktree story alone is worth the switch.

Grok Bot landed the best design yet — I wrote it up at [blog.duyet.net/2026/08/grok-bot](https://blog.duyet.net/2026/08/grok-bot/). They move fast and fix a lot, and it works effectively, not aspirationally. No FOMO here.

anyrouter CLI — I keep trying to make this mainstream, and it's not good enough honestly. The upstream providers are still unstable, and finding more good sources is the actual bottleneck. Spell it right, too: anyrouter, not anyroouter.

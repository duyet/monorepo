People keep asking how to trust agents more. Most still review every line. That is the wrong lever.

[@poteto](https://x.com/silentguyy66/status/2099094124628492717): ban the patterns agents are bad at, put verification in front, then let them merge. CI, tests, skills, cloud agents that open and ship PRs. You stop cooking every dish. You run the kitchen.

That is how I trust Grok Bot / duyetbot / [herdr-desk](https://github.com/duyet/herdr-desk) more than line-by-line review. I wrote the longer version at [Grok Bot](https://blog.duyet.net/2026/08/grok-bot/). Same pipeline: Verification → skills → cloud agents → automerged PRs. Hire another reviewer, or spend the tokens on checks that actually catch what agents mess up.
